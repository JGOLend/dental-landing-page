Frequently asked questions

Can I finance the equipment as well as the fit-out?

Yes. Depending on the lender and your circumstances, finance can potentially cover dental equipment, fit-out costs and other practice setup expenses.

Can I buy equipment for an existing practice?

Yes. We can look at finance for replacing or upgrading existing equipment, including major purchases such as chairs, imaging and sterilisation equipment.

I'm starting a new practice - can you help with the whole setup?

Yes. We can look at the overall funding requirement rather than just one piece of equipment, including equipment, fit-out and other eligible setup costs.

Do I need a 20% deposit?

Not necessarily. The deposit or equity contribution required depends on the lender, the asset, your financial position and the overall deal.

I already have a bank. Can you still help?

Absolutely. We can compare options across our lender panel rather than assuming your existing bank is the best option.

Will applying affect my credit file?

We can discuss your circumstances and the appropriate options before proceeding with an application. No need to blindly apply everywhere.
